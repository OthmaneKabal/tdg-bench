# TDGBench data licensing

TDGBench uses file-level licensing; the dataset is not covered by one blanket
licence.

## CC BY 4.0: TDGBench graph contributions

The authors' original contributions to the following materials are released
under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY
4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/):

- GT2KG and KG-GEN graph files and their preprocessing variants;
- graph packaging and associated benchmark metadata;
- the ten benchmark split indices; and
- TDGBench-created annotations and selections.

Attribution must credit TDGBench, link to CC BY 4.0, and indicate changes.
This grant applies only to the authors' copyrightable contributions; it does
not grant rights in third-party content or identifiers incorporated in a file.

## Material under separate terms

- **Software and the `tdg-bench` library:** MIT.
- **Mapped MedMentions text corpus:** CC0 1.0 under the upstream MedMentions
  dedication when included in a release.
- **UMLS material:** not redistributed. Access, reconstruction and
  redistribution of UMLS-derived content are governed by the UMLS licence.
